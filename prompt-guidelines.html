Prompt Guidelines

Tips for writing effective prompts

•

Be clear and specific

State exactly what you want. Vague prompts lead to vague answers.

Instead of:

"Tell me about dogs"

Try:

"List 5 dog breeds suitable for apartments, including their size and energy level"

•

Provide context

Share relevant background information so the AI understands your situation.

Instead of:

"Write an email"

Try:

"Write a professional email to my manager requesting time off next Friday for a medical appointment"

•

Specify the format

Tell the AI how you want the response structured.

Examples:

  • "Respond in bullet points"
  • "Give me a table with columns for X, Y, Z"
  • "Keep it under 100 words"
  • "Format as a numbered list"
•

Use examples

Show what you want by providing examples of good output.

Example prompt:

"Generate product names like these: 'CloudSync Pro', 'DataFlow Elite', 'SwiftDeploy'. Give me 5 more for a project management tool."

•

Break down complex tasks

Split large requests into smaller, manageable steps.

Instead of:

"Build me a complete marketing strategy"

Try:

"Step 1: Identify my target audience for a fitness app aimed at beginners"

"Step 2: Suggest 3 marketing channels for reaching them"

•

Assign a role

Give the AI a persona to shape the response style and expertise.

Examples:

  • "Act as a senior software engineer reviewing this code"
  • "You are a friendly tutor explaining calculus to a high schooler"
  • "Respond as a professional copywriter"
•

Iterate and refine

Don't expect perfection on the first try. Build on responses.

Follow-up examples:

  • "Make it more formal"
  • "Expand on point 3"
  • "Simplify this for a non-technical audience"
  • "Add more specific examples"
